The problem

Most businesses are data-rich and insight-poor

Spreadsheets pile up. Dashboards go stale. By the time a problem is noticed by a human, it's already a quarter old.

No central analytics platform

Data sits scattered across spreadsheets, tools and databases with nothing tying it together.

Spreadsheets, not signal

Rows and columns don't tell a story on their own — someone still has to interpret them.

No predictive insight

Most reporting looks backward. Few teams can see demand or revenue before it happens.

Anomalies go unnoticed

A sales drop or a fraud pattern can run for weeks before anyone spots it manually.

No automated reporting

Reports are rebuilt by hand every cycle instead of generating themselves.

No data science on staff

Smaller teams rarely have access to the modelling expertise larger companies take for granted.

SmartBI replaces all six gaps with one automated, AI-driven analytics layer that sits on top of the data you already have.

System architecture

Five layers, one continuous pipeline

Every dataset travels the same path — from raw upload to a recommendation you can act on. Click a layer to open it.

01

Data Collection

Pulls data in from CSV and Excel uploads, PostgreSQL, MySQL and MongoDB connections, or any REST API — whatever your business already uses to store information.

02

Data Processing

Validates schema, fixes missing values, removes duplicates, handles outliers and engineers features so every downstream model gets clean input.

03

Machine Learning Engine

Runs clustering, forecasting (Prophet, ARIMA, LSTM) and anomaly-detection models against the cleaned dataset.

04

Business Intelligence Engine

Converts raw model output into KPIs, plain-English insights and answers to natural-language questions.

05

Visualization & Reporting

Renders dashboards, sends alerts and exports executive, sales, forecast and anomaly reports on demand.
